What the AeroTwin Model Does

📡

10 Hz Multi-Sensor Telemetry

Generates synchronized RPM, CHT, EGT, oil pressure, fuel flow, and vibration signals with physics correlations matching future CAN/ECU payloads.

🔬

MVEM Physics Twin Model

Mean Value Engine Model computes theoretical expected states and calculates exact physics residuals ($\Delta S = \text{Actual} - \text{Expected}$).

📈

AI Fault Diagnostics & RUL

Isolation Forest anomaly detection, XGBoost fault classification, and RUL estimation with 80% prediction uncertainty bounds.

🧬

SHAP Feature Attribution

Explains fault root causes by outputting quantitative attribution weights for maintainers instead of opaque classification labels.

🎲

500-Run Monte Carlo Simulator

Simulates mission completion probability under high-altitude, hot-ambient, and severe throttle envelope perturbations.

🔒

HMAC-SHA256 Audit Trail

Append-only Merkle hash chain records all telemetry steps, EKF estimates, and diagnostic alerts with tamper detection.
